Forgot your email?

Peyper-Trails logs you in with the email address you signed up with. For your safety we can't look emails up on this screen, because that would also let strangers find other people's emails. Here is how to find yours:

  1. Search your inboxes. Look for an email from Peyper-Trails (we sent a confirmation link when you signed up). The address it was sent to is your login email.
  2. Check your saved logins. Your browser or phone may have saved it. In Chrome, open the menu, tap Password Manager and search for “peyper”.
  3. Try your other emails. Tap Forgot password? on the login screen and enter each address. The one with an account will get a reset email.

If that email is gone for good: your account can't be recovered, because there is no other way to prove it is yours and no admin can reach it. If your login email is a Gmail address, try Continue with Google on the login screen.
